## ⚠️ Risk Warnings

### Financial Risks:
- **Market Volatility:** Prices can fluctuate dramatically
- **Platform Risk:** Exchanges can fail or be hacked
- **Regulatory Risk:** Laws can change affecting your holdings
- **Liquidity Risk:** May not be able to sell at desired price
- **Bonus Risk:** Bonuses may have withdrawal restrictions

### Investment-Specific Risks:
- **Trading Losses:** You can lose your entire investment
- **Market Manipulation:** Crypto markets can be manipulated
- **Technical Issues:** Platform outages during critical times
- **Counterparty Risk:** Platform may not honor obligations

### Personal Financial Risks:
- **Tax Liabilities:** Trading profits are taxable
- **Financial Overextension:** Don't borrow money to trade
- **Emotional Trading:** Avoid decisions based on fear or greed
- **Time Commitment:** Active trading requires significant time
