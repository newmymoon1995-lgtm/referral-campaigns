## ⚠️ Risk Warnings

### App-Specific Risks:
- **Data Privacy:** Apps may collect extensive personal data
- **In-App Purchases:** Easy to overspend on microtransactions
- **Time Investment:** Some tasks are more time-consuming than expected
- **Platform Changes:** Apps can change terms without notice
- **Account Security:** Risk of account compromise

### Financial Risks:
- **Required Deposits:** May not be recoverable
- **Withdrawal Restrictions:** Bonuses may have conditions
- **Exchange Rate Risks:** Currency conversion fees
- **Payment Processing:** Delays or issues with payments

### Personal Data Risks:
- **KYC Requirements:** Extensive personal information required
- **Data Sharing:** Information may be shared with third parties
- **Profile Building:** Apps build detailed user profiles
- **Location Tracking:** Some apps require location access
