## 🔍 Before You Participate

### Travel-Specific Considerations:
1. **Booking Terms:** Understand cancellation and refund policies
2. **Geographic Restrictions:** Check if service available in your region
3. **Currency Exchange:** Consider exchange rates and fees
4. **Travel Dates:** Bonus may require specific booking timing
5. **Service Quality:** Research platform reputation and reliability

### Lifestyle-Specific Considerations:
1. **Product Quality:** Understand what you're purchasing
2. **Return Policies:** Check return and refund options
3. **Shipping:** Consider delivery times and costs
4. **Platform Trust:** Research seller and platform reputation
5. **Payment Security:** Ensure secure payment processing

### Due Diligence Checklist:
- [ ] Read platform reviews and ratings
- [ ] Check cancellation and refund policies
- [ ] Verify geographic availability
- [ ] Understand all fees and charges

## ⚠️ Risk Warnings

### Travel-Specific Risks:
- **Booking Changes:** Plans may need to change, consider flexibility
- **Cancellation Fees:** Some bookings have strict cancellation policies
- **Service Disruptions:** Transportation can be delayed or cancelled
- **Currency Fluctuation:** Exchange rates can affect costs
- **Platform Reliability:** Technical issues can affect bookings

### Lifestyle Shopping Risks:
- **Product Quality:** Items may not meet expectations
- **Shipping Issues:** Delays or problems with delivery
- **Return Complications:** Return processes can be difficult
- **Payment Security:** Risk of fraudulent transactions
- **Platform Changes:** Terms can change unexpectedly

### Financial Risks:
- **Required Purchases:** May need to spend money to qualify
- **Withdrawal Restrictions:** Credits may have usage limitations
- **Exchange Rate Risks:** Currency conversion fees
- **Hidden Fees:** Additional charges not immediately apparent
