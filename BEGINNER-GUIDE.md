- **Use official apps only** - avoid third-party sites
- **Don't use VPNs** unless explicitly allowed
- **Have your documents ready** - clear photos speed up verification
- **Start with small amounts** - test the platform first

### During the Process:
- **Take screenshots** of each step for reference
- **Note confirmation numbers** for support if needed
- **Check email for verification codes** promptly
- **Don't skip any steps** - follow instructions exactly
- **Be patient with verification** - some take time

### After Completion:
- **Withdraw your initial deposit** if possible

- **Monitor for any required follow-up actions**
- **Share your experience** to help others

## ⚠️ Common Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Using VPN When Prohibited
- **Problem:** Many platforms block VPN usage
- **Solution:** Use your real location and IP address
- **Consequence:** Account suspension or bonus denial

### Mistake 2: Skipping KYC Verification
- **Problem:** Trying to avoid identity verification
- **Solution:** Complete KYC honestly and completely
- **Consequence:** Cannot withdraw bonuses or funds

### Mistake 3: Not Reading Terms
- **Problem:** Missing specific requirements
- **Solution:** Read all terms before starting
- **Consequence:** Wasted time and deposits

### Mistake 4: Using Fake Information
- **Problem:** Providing false personal details
- **Solution:** Use real, accurate information
- **Consequence:** Permanent account ban

### Mistake 5: Depositing More Than Required
- **Problem:** Depositing large amounts unnecessarily
- **Solution:** Stick to minimum required deposits
- **Consequence:** Unnecessary risk exposure
