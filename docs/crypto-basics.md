# 🪙 Cryptocurrency Basics

Essential knowledge for understanding cryptocurrency-related referral programs and making informed decisions in the crypto space.

## ⚠️ Important Disclaimer

**Cryptocurrency is highly volatile and risky. This information is for educational purposes only. Never invest more than you can afford to lose, and always conduct thorough research before making any crypto-related decisions.**

## 🏗️ Understanding Cryptocurrency

### What is Cryptocurrency?

**Definition:**
Digital or virtual currency that uses cryptography for security and operates on decentralized networks called blockchains.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Decentralized (no central authority)
- Cryptographically secured
- Limited supply (for most cryptocurrencies)
- Global accessibility
- Pseudonymous transactions

### Major Cryptocurrencies

**Bitcoin (BTC):**
- First and largest cryptocurrency
- Digital gold/store of value
- Limited supply (21 million coins)
- High market capitalization

**Ethereum (ETH):**
- Smart contract platform
- Enables decentralized applications
- More flexible than Bitcoin
- Second largest by market cap

**Stablecoins:**
- Pegged to fiat currencies (USD, EUR)
- Less volatile than other cryptocurrencies
- Used for trading and transfers
- Examples: USDT, USDC, DAI

## 🔐 Wallet Security

### Types of Wallets

**Hot Wallets (Online):**
- Convenient for frequent trading
- Connected to the internet
- Higher security risk
- Examples: Exchange wallets, mobile wallets

**Cold Wallets (Offline):**
- Maximum security
- Not connected to internet
- Less convenient for trading
- Examples: Hardware wallets, paper wallets

### Security Best Practices

**Essential Practices:**
- Use hardware wallets for significant holdings
- Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)
- Never share private keys or seed phrases
- Keep software and firmware updated
- Use reputable wallets and exchanges

**Red Flags:**
- Requests for your private keys
- Promises of guaranteed returns
- Pressure to send crypto quickly
- Unofficial wallet downloads
- Poor grammar in communications

## 🏪 Cryptocurrency Exchanges

### Types of Exchanges

**Centralized Exchanges (CEX):**
- Operated by companies
- Require KYC verification
- Offer fiat on-ramps/off-ramps
- Examples: Binance, Coinbase, Kraken

**Decentralized Exchanges (DEX):**
- No central authority
- Often no KYC required
- Use smart contracts
- Examples: Uniswap, PancakeSwap

### Exchange Security

**Security Measures:**
- Most funds in cold storage
- Insurance coverage
- Security audits
- Bug bounty programs
- Regulatory compliance

**Risk Factors:**
- Exchange hacks (historical precedent)
- Regulatory changes
- Platform insolvency
- Withdrawal limits
- Technical issues

## 📊 Trading Basics

### Understanding Orders

**Market Orders:**
- Execute immediately at current price
- No price guarantee
- Good for liquid markets
- May experience slippage

**Limit Orders:**
- Execute only at specified price or better
- Price guarantee
- May not execute immediately
- Good for controlling entry/exit points

**Stop-Loss Orders:**
- Automatically sell if price drops below level
- Risk management tool
- Can protect against significant losses
- May trigger at unfavorable prices during volatility

### Trading Volume

**What It Means:**
- Total value of trades in a period
- Indicator of market activity
- Often required for bonuses
- Calculated as: Price × Quantity

**Example:**
- Buy 0.5 BTC at $50,000 = $25,000 volume
- Sell 0.5 BTC at $51,000 = $25,500 volume
- Total volume: $50,500

## 🌐 On-Ramps and Off-Ramps

### Fiat to Crypto (On-Ramps)

**Methods:**
- Bank transfers (SEPA, SWIFT)
- Credit/debit cards
- PayPal (some exchanges)
- P2P trading platforms

**Considerations:**
- Processing times vary
- Fees differ by method
- Some methods have limits
- KYC requirements often apply

### Crypto to Fiat (Off-Ramps)

**Methods:**
- Bank withdrawals
- Crypto debit cards
- P2P selling
- PayPal withdrawals (limited)

**Considerations:**
- Withdrawal limits
- Processing times
- Exchange rates
- Tax implications

## ⚖️ Regulation and Compliance

### KYC/AML Requirements

**Know Your Customer (KYC):**
- Identity verification required by most exchanges
- Document submission (ID, passport, proof of address)
- Prevents money laundering and fraud
- Required by regulated exchanges

**Anti-Money Laundering (AML):**
- Monitoring of suspicious transactions
- Reporting requirements for exchanges
- Travel rule implementation
- Compliance with international standards

### Geographic Restrictions

**Common Restrictions:**
- US residents often excluded from crypto bonuses
- Some countries have strict crypto regulations
- State-level restrictions within countries
- Evolving regulatory landscape

**Why Restrictions Exist:**
- Consumer protection
- Financial stability concerns
- AML/CFT compliance
- Tax collection
- Capital controls

## 🎯 Risk Management

### Volatility Risk

**Understanding Volatility:**
- Crypto prices can change dramatically
- 10-20% daily moves are common
- Longer-term trends can be unpredictable
- Historical performance doesn't guarantee future results

**Managing Volatility:**
- Only invest what you can afford to lose
- Diversify across different cryptocurrencies
- Use dollar-cost averaging
- Set stop-loss levels
- Avoid emotional trading

### Platform Risk

**Exchange Risks:**
- Exchange insolvency (historical examples)
- Security breaches
- Regulatory actions
- Technical failures
- Withdrawal restrictions

**Mitigation Strategies:**
- Don't keep all crypto on exchanges
- Use reputable, regulated exchanges
- Enable all security features
- Keep records of all transactions
- Have backup plans

## ⚠️ Common Scams

### Red Flags

**Investment Scams:**
- Guaranteed high returns
- Pressure to invest quickly
- Requests to send crypto to unknown addresses
- Unrealistic referral programs
- Pyramid scheme structures

**Phishing Attempts:**
- Fake websites mimicking real exchanges
- Emails requesting login information
- Fake wallet downloads
- Social media impersonation
- Malicious browser extensions

**How to Protect Yourself:**
- Always verify URLs (look for HTTPS)
- Never share private keys or seed phrases
- Use official app stores for wallet apps
- Enable 2FA everywhere possible
- Be skeptical of unrealistic promises

**Before Participating in Crypto Programs:**

4. Consider regulatory implications

**Risk Assessment:**
